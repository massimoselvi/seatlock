## Summary

The first project week delivered the whole product and its operating documents. All four planned milestones are closed, and a security milestone was added and closed. The seat guarantee is proved by a load test over real HTTP: exactly 10 confirmed in 8 of 8 runs, and up to 24 when the lock is removed. The main open point is the cloud: nothing has been deployed, and the dry run waits on the sandbox account login. This report also introduces the project documents. The sponsor must approve the baseline so v0.6 can be tracked.
